# TAIAO v0.1 — Game Design Notes

## Core fantasy

The player has a small digital companion that notices them, remembers them and grows through repeated care.

## Prototype stats

- Hunger: decreases over time; feeding restores it.
- Happiness: affected by play and exploration.
- Energy: spent on play/exploration and restored by rest.

## Māori language layer

The prototype uses a very small set of words as placeholders:

- Kia ora — hello / acknowledgement
- Ka pai — good / well done
- Whāngai — feed / nourish
- Tākaro — play
- Okioki — rest
- Tūhura — explore
- Te ngahere — the forest
- Ngā manu — the birds

These should be reviewed by qualified te reo Māori language and cultural advisors before public or commercial use.

## Future systems

Potential future additions:

- companion personality traits
- weather and seasons
- richer te reo interactions
- sound and music
- multiple environments
- friendship / relationship progression
- quests and discovery
- whānau mode
